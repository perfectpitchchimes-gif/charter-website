Yellowfin tuna.

Thunnus albacares

A streamlined ocean traveller, dark above and bright along its sides. Yellow fins and a row of small finlets lead back to the deeply forked tail.

In the water

Yellowfin tuna range through tropical and subtropical oceans, usually in the upper water. They can cross entire oceans; young fish often school with other tuna.

They hunt fish, squid and crustaceans. The long yellow fins and small yellow finlets are useful features for recognising them.

Natural history: NOAA Fisheries.

In Charter

The heaviest of the five fish at the prototype’s default sizes. Its weight carries through from the line to the deck and into the catch you keep.
