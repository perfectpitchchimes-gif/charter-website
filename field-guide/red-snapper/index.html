Red snapper.

Lutjanus campechanus

A deep-bodied fish washed in red, with a pointed snout and red eyes. Older fish spend much of their time around hard structure.

In the water

Red snapper live in warmer western Atlantic waters. Young fish use sandy and muddy bottoms; adults tend to stay close to reefs, rocks, ledges and other structure on the continental shelf.

Their diet includes fish, shrimp, crabs and squid. The pointed face ends in a mouth with prominent canine teeth.

Natural history: NOAA Fisheries.

In Charter

The snapper’s deep body gives it a different shape to handle at the board. In the current prototype, it is prepared in two sections, one on each side.
