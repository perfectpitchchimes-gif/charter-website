Mahi-mahi.

Coryphaena hippurus

A long dorsal fin runs nearly the length of this bright ocean fish. Greens, blues and golds shift across its flanks.

In the water

Mahi-mahi live near the surface in tropical and subtropical seas. Smaller fish are often found around floating objects and patches of Sargassum; large males also range through open water.

They grow quickly and hunt small fish and invertebrates near the surface. Their vivid colours fade rapidly after death.

Natural history: NOAA Fisheries.

In Charter

A long-bodied catch with a tall dorsal fin. Like the other fish in Charter, it can be brought aboard, handled on deck and worked by hand with the knife.
