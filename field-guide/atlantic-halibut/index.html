Atlantic halibut.

Hippoglossus hippoglossus

An immense flatfish with both eyes on its upper side. Its dark surface disappears against the seabed; the pale underside stays hidden below.

In the water

Atlantic halibut live close to sand, gravel and clay seabeds in the northern Atlantic. Their range extends from coastal waters out to the upper continental slope.

Small halibut feed mainly on invertebrates. As they grow, fish make up more of their diet. They are the largest living flatfish species.

Natural history: NOAA Fisheries.

In Charter

The broad, flat body changes the preparation: there are four fillet sections, two on each face. Turn the fish over to work on the other side.
