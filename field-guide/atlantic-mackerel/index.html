Atlantic mackerel.

Scomber scombrus

A restless shoaling fish, blue-green above and silver below. Dark, wavering bars break up its back; the flanks flash as the school turns.

In the water

Atlantic mackerel travel in schools through cold and temperate North Atlantic waters. They are often near the surface above the continental shelf, moving between spawning and summer feeding grounds.

They feed on small crustaceans, squid and fish.

Natural history: NOAA Fisheries.

In Charter

One of the smaller catches aboard Charter. Its narrow body can be steadied on the cutting board while you work along each side with the knife.
