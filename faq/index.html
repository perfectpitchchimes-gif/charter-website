Questions about Charter.

Can I play Charter solo?

Yes. Solo play is part of the plan, alongside playing with friends. The current prototype can also be played alone.

How many players can join?

The plan is 1–8 players in total. The current private prototype supports up to three players while multiplayer development continues.

What platforms is Charter planned for?

Windows PC. Other platforms haven’t been announced.

When can I play?

Charter is in development and being tested privately. The prototype includes fishing, boat handling and catch preparation, but the full game is still being built. There is no announced release date or public download yet.

For progress on the game, read the development posts.
