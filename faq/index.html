Questions about Charter.

What kind of game is Charter?

Charter is a co-operative fishing game. You and your friends share one working boat on a windy stretch of coast, take her out to find fish, and bring the catch aboard to be filleted and sold. Between trips you can walk around the harbour towns or take the boat somewhere you haven’t been.

Can I play Charter solo?

Yes. Solo play is part of the plan, alongside playing with friends. The current build can also be played alone.

How many players can join?

The plan is 1–8 players in total. The current private test build supports up to three players while multiplayer development continues.

How do friends join my game?

In the current test build you invite a friend through Steam, and they can come aboard while a trip is already under way.

What platforms is Charter planned for?

Windows PC. Other platforms haven’t been announced.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Every action in the game can be done on a controller, and mouse and keyboard work as well.

What kinds of fishing are there?

Most fish are caught on a rod and line. You can also set pots for lobster and crab, dive with a spear, and pick shellfish by hand on a dive or in the shallows. A few species are protected, so those go back over the side after you land them.

Are the fish real species?

Yes. The fish are real species, and each one has a field guide entry about where it lives and how it behaves. Five of those entries are in the field guide on this site.

Do I have to fish, or can I just explore?

You can explore. The coast is open from the start, with harbour towns to walk around, buildings you can go into and reefs you can dive on, and you can leave the rods alone for as long as you like.

Is there weather, and day and night?

Yes. The weather runs from flat calm to storms, and the day runs through to night. Both change how the boat handles and how much you can see, so the lighthouses and harbour lamps matter after dark.

When can I play, and what will it cost?

Charter is in development by VexingImp and Charter Games for Windows PC, planned for Steam Early Access. A Steam Coming Soon page is on the way, with a playable demo planned for Steam Next Fest in February 2027. Price hasn’t been announced yet.

How do I hear about a playtest or demo?

Playtests and demo dates will be announced on the accounts listed on the links page and in the development posts on this site.

For progress on the game, read the development posts.
