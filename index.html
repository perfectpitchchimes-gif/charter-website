Fishing, and everything
around it.

One working boat, a small crew, and a windy stretch of coast.

Charter is a co-operative fishing game for solo players or small crews of friends. Take the wheel out of Greywick, read the water and the chart, and land real marine species by rod, pot, or spear. Fish have weight, keep thrashing across the planks, and need a steady hand at the cutting board.

Some hours are quiet at the rail. Others go sideways when something heavy hits the line or the sea gets up.
