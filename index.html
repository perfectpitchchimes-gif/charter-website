Fishing, and everything
around it.

A boat, a small crew, and a stretch of coastal water.

Charter is a fishing game in development for Windows, planned for solo play and crews of up to eight. Take the helm, watch for activity on the water, and bring your catch aboard. Fish have weight, keep moving on deck, and need a steady hand at the cutting board.

There’s room to work together, get in each other’s way, or leave the rod alone for a while.
